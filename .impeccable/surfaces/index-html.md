# Surface: home (index.html)

Mode: Experience (personal portfolio / resume site). Build path: code-led (no image generation).

Audience: internship screeners from electrical design-build firms and from tech/hardware employers, equally. Job: understand within seconds who Marcus is and that he's credible on both sides, then contact him or download the resume.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, self-hosted fonts, AA contrast, reduced motion, phone-first usable.
Owner-pinned brief (2026-11-08), latest: "make it more professional with this color scheme: #213885, #ECDFD2, #5F3475, #081849, #CCCACC, #893172". This builds on the earlier pinned "cloudy vibe" brief. No botanical or aloha motifs (rejected).

## Direction contract

THESIS: An evening sky, restrained: a navy-to-royal-blue header with slow, low-contrast mist, a warm cream reading field for the resume, and the night sky again at the close. Professional first; the cloud is atmosphere, never decoration that competes with the content.

OWN-WORLD: The owner's palette exactly. Navy #081849 is ink and the dark grounds. Royal #213885 marks rules, links, the active nav and key numbers. Cream #ECDFD2 is the page ground, with lifted-cream paper cards (#F6EFE7). Magenta #893172 is the only action colour (primary button, "now", the copied state). Plum #5F3475 marks dates and labels. Gray #CCCACC is for hairlines. Headings use an editorial grotesk (Schibsted Grotesk); body is a clean sans (Figtree). Corners are tight (8–12px), shadows are soft, and nothing is a pill except nothing.

STORY: The visitor sees a person who has already worked on a real high-rise's electrical design-build team and also writes code. They skim both sides in 30 seconds, then email him or take the resume.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width navy-to-royal header. A cream nav bar with an MG mark sits top center. At top left, the name is set large in cream, then the role, a "From Kailua, HI · now in Santa Clara, CA" line, the intro, and actions: Email me (magenta) and Resume PDF (cream). Faint mist drifts across the lower header, behind the two "In the field" / "In code" cards.

FORM: Owner-pinned direction (palette and "more professional"), so concept-seed was not rerun; the earlier roll was seed:ebdcd83e64a33adf, superseded by the owner's briefs. Signature interaction: a royal-blue indicator in the nav slides to the current section. Motion grammar: very slow linear mist drift, UI on a strong ease-out under 260ms, press feedback on controls.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
