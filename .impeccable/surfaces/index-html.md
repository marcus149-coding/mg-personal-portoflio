# Surface: home (index.html)

Mode: Experience (personal portfolio / resume site). Build path: code-led (no image generation).

Audience: internship screeners from electrical design-build firms and from tech/hardware employers, equally. Job: understand within seconds who Marcus is and that he's credible on both sides, then contact him or download the resume.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, self-hosted fonts, AA contrast, reduced motion, phone-first usable.
Owner-pinned briefs, latest first: (2026-11-12) "change the color scheme to black and white"; (2026-11-12) "add in speckled graphite and fluted glass details as well as contour lines"; (2026-11-08) "make it more professional", with a six-colour palette now retired by the black-and-white brief. Builds on the earlier pinned "cloudy vibe" brief. No botanical or aloha motifs (rejected).

## Direction contract

THESIS: An evening sky in black and white: a speckled black-to-charcoal header traced with contour lines and slow, low-contrast mist, fluted glass over it, a light-gray reading field for the resume, and black again at the close. Professional first; the materials are atmosphere, never decoration that competes with the content.

OWN-WORLD: No hue. Ink #0a0a0a is text, dark grounds, structure and actions on light. Light #f4f4f4 is the page ground and actions on the sky, with white #ffffff paper cards. Meta gray #555555 marks dates and labels. Gray #cccccc is for hairlines. Graphite #1c1c1c is the speckled slab. Materials: speckled graphite, topographic contour lines, fluted glass (see DESIGN.md, Materials). Headings use an editorial grotesk (Schibsted Grotesk); body is a clean sans (Figtree). Corners are tight (8–12px), shadows are soft, and nothing is a pill.

STORY: The visitor sees a person who has already worked on a real high-rise's electrical design-build team and also writes code. They skim both sides in 30 seconds, then email him or take the resume.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width black-to-charcoal header with contour lines opening to the right. A white nav bar with a speckled graphite MG mark sits top center. At top left, the name is set large in light gray, then the role, a "From Kailua, HI · now in Santa Clara, CA" line, the intro, and actions: Email me (solid light) and Resume PDF (outline). Faint mist drifts across the lower header, behind the two fluted-glass "In the field" / "In code" cards.

FORM: Owner-pinned direction (black and white, materials, "more professional"), so concept-seed was not rerun; the earlier roll was seed:ebdcd83e64a33adf, superseded by the owner's briefs. Signature interaction: a black indicator in the nav slides to the current section. Motion grammar: very slow linear mist drift, UI on a strong ease-out under 260ms, press feedback on controls.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
