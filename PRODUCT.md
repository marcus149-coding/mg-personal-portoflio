## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (index.html, style.css, script.js), no build step, so it can be served as-is from GitHub Pages or any static host. Deploy target not yet confirmed; the owner delegated extras ("add whatever you think is best").

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers and engineers screening internship candidates, split equally between two worlds:
- electrical contracting / design-build firms (like Rosendin Electric), who care about construction-document fluency: submittals, BIM, single-line diagrams, RFIs, BOMs, PCOs;
- tech / hardware employers, who care about programming, projects and ECE coursework.

They typically arrive from a resume, LinkedIn or an application link, skim for under a minute, and decide whether to reach out.

## Product Purpose

A personal site for Marcus Gomez, an Electrical & Computer Engineering student at Santa Clara University (Class of 2029). Success is when a screener understands within seconds who he is and that he's credible on both sides (field engineering and code), then contacts him or saves his resume.
